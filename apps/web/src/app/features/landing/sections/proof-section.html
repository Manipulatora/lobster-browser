Why it holds up

Detection rarely catches a fingerprint. It catches the seam where a spoof leaks — a value that arrived from a script, or a device that contradicts itself. Lobster removes that surface: the identity is applied natively inside the engine, and every signal is reconciled against the others before the profile is allowed to launch.
